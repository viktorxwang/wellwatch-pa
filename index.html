    About the data. Well locations, depths and hazard readings in this prototype are synthetic. They are generated to match the documented distribution of Pennsylvania's legacy wells. They cluster in the Venango, Warren and McKean oil region, where commercial drilling began at Titusville in 1859. Pennsylvania has roughly 27,000 documented orphan and abandoned wells, with credible estimates of 100,000 to 560,000 more that were never recorded. Production deployment would read the DEP Oil & Gas Locations feed and write reports back through the DEP eFACTS intake.
